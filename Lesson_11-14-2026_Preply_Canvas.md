## Block 2: The 45-Second Elevator Pitch (Abitur Outline)
```text
⏱️ 45-SECOND ELEVATOR PITCH / ABITUR OUTLINE
Rule: Present tense • Objective • Zero personal opinion!

1. HOOK & PREMISE (0–15s):
   "At its core, [Title] is a [genre] that depicts / examines..."
2. CONTRAST & CONFLICT (15–30s):
   "The narrative juxtaposes [initial expectation] with [harsh reality]."
3. CENTRAL MECHANISM (30–45s):
   "Ultimately, the work exposes how [propaganda / war / peer pressure]..."

🎯 C1 Verbs: depicts • juxtaposes • dismantles • exposes • satirizes • underscores
```
