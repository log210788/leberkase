## Part 1: "Denglish Buster" & Lesson 01 Recall
Upgrade each sentence from literal German structure (*Denglish*) to natural British English:

1. *"I ate my breakfast ten minutes ago."*  
   → *"I've just _______________________ breakfast."*
2. *"Through discuss about problems, we find solutions."*  
   → *"By _______________________ problems, we find solutions."* *(Careful: does `discuss` take `about`?)*
3. *"The students doesn't want to speak English in class."*  
   → *"Most of the students _______________________ really want to speak English."*
4. *"If you are not self-conscious enough, your teammates push you."* *(False friend: `selbstbewusst`)*  
   → *"If you lack _______________________, your teammates push you forward."*
5. *"I could talk a long while about geopolitics."*  
   → *"I could talk about geopolitics for _______________________."*
6. *"Summarizing a story in the time it takes to write an elevator."*  
   → *"Summarizing a story in the time it takes to _______________________ an elevator."*

## Part 2: The 45-Second *Elevator Pitch* (Abitur *Outline* Blueprint)
An Abitur **Outline** and an **Elevator Pitch** share the same rule: **present tense, strictly objective, zero rambling**.

### The 3-Beat Formula:
- **Beat 1 — Hook & Core Premise (0–15s):**  
  *"At its core, **[Title]** is a **[genre]** by **[Author/Director]** that examines / depicts..."*
- **Beat 2 — Structural Contrast / Conflict (15–30s):**  
  *"The narrative juxtaposes **[initial expectation / propaganda]** with **[harsh reality / satirical outcome]**."*
- **Beat 3 — Central Mechanism (30–45s):**  
  *"Ultimately, the work exposes how **[propaganda / peer pressure / war]** shapes human behavior."*

**C1 Power Verbs (Use at least 3):**  
`depicts` • `juxtaposes` • `dismantles` • `exposes` • `satirizes` • `underscores`

## Part 3: The Abitur *Comment* & Geopolitics Debate Arena
Use the **C1 Concession Flip** to sound analytical and balanced before delivering your main point:

| Move | C1 Frame |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Concession (Acknowledge the other side)** | *"While there is some merit to the view that..."* / *"Admittedly, on the surface..."* |
| **2. The Flip (Pivot to your thesis)** | *"...that said, looking beneath the surface..."* / *"...however, it would be short-sighted to underestimate..."* |
| **3. The Clincher (Final takeaway)** | *"At its core, the issue boils down to..."* / *"Ultimately, the decisive factor is..."* |

### Choose 2 Debate Prompts (90 Seconds Each):
- **Option A (Cinema & Propaganda):** Which leaves a deeper impact on an audience: the raw trench tragedy of *All Quiet on the Western Front* or the satirical, tongue-in-cheek parody of *Starship Troopers* / *Grand Theft Auto*?
- **Option B (Geopolitics & Social Media):** In an age of algorithms and echo chambers, is modern youth more vulnerable to peer pressure and political propaganda than earlier generations?
- **Option C (Abitur & Education):** Do 200-minute written exams truly measure ability, or should schools test high-pressure communication and problem-solving instead?
